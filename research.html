Breast Ultrasound AI Report Generation

Developed a deep‑learning system that combines CNN image features (ResNet101) with a Transformer encoder‑decoder to generate radiology‑style reports from breast ultrasound images.

Used Grad‑CAM and attention visualisation to support explainability. Evaluated generated text with BLEU, CIDEr, METEOR and ROUGE metrics.

Dataset: 256 breast ultrasound scans (benign, malignant, normal) with associated radiology information and reports.

This is a research prototype demonstrating feasibility, not a clinical diagnostic tool.

Tools: Python, PyTorch, CNNs, Transformers, computer vision, explainable AI
